The iterator related intrinsics: the array iterator (%ArrayIteratorPrototype%), and the generator machinery %GeneratorFunction%, %GeneratorFunction.prototype% and %GeneratorPrototype% with next, return and throw (ECMA-262 6th edition 25.1, 25.2, 25.3).
