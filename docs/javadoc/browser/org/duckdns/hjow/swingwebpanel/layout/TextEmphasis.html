Class TextEmphasis

java.lang.Object
  org.duckdns.hjow.swingwebpanel.layout.TextEmphasis

public final class TextEmphasis
extends java.lang.Object

The emphasis marks of a style (CSS Text Decoration Module Level 4, https://www.w3.org/TR/css-text-decor-4/#emphasis-marks, W3C Working Draft 4 May 2022): text-emphasis-style (a filled or open shape: dot, circle, double-circle, triangle, sesame, or a string), text-emphasis-color and text-emphasis-position (over or under the text in horizontal text). A mark is drawn once for each typographic character unit at 50 percent of the font size, centered on the character, except for separators (Z*), punctuation (P*, with a few exceptions such as # and %) and control, format and unassigned characters (marked(int)). The room for the marks is added to the line like the room of ruby text: reserve is the height that is added above (over) or below (under) the text fragment. The marks of vertical text are not drawn.

Usage: TextEmphasis e = TextEmphasis.of(style); if (e != null) { ... e.getMark() ... }.

스타일의 강조점(CSS Text Decoration Module Level 4, W3C 작업 초안 2022-05-04): text-emphasis-style(채운 또는 빈 모양: dot, circle, double-circle, triangle, sesame, 또는 문자열), text-emphasis-color, text-emphasis-position(가로 텍스트에서 텍스트의 위(over) 또는 아래(under)). 점은 글자 단위마다 한 번, 글꼴 크기의 50 % 로 글자 가운데에 그리며 구분자(Z*), 문장 부호(P*. #, % 등 몇 가지 예외 제외), 제어/서식/미할당 문자에는 그리지 않는다(marked(int)). 점의 공간은 ruby 텍스트의 공간처럼 줄에 더해진다: reserve 는 텍스트 조각의 위(over) 또는 아래(under)에 더하는 높이이다. 세로쓰기 텍스트의 점은 그리지 않는다.

사용법: TextEmphasis e = TextEmphasis.of(style); if (e != null) { ... e.getMark() ... }.

Returns the baseline of the marks of a text fragment. The marks stand in the room that reserve gave: over the text, just above its ascent (the part that the text font itself needs; the room of the marks is on top of it), and under the text, just below its descent.
