org.duckdns.hjow.swingwebpanel.layout
Class Effects

java.lang.Object
  org.duckdns.hjow.swingwebpanel.layout.Effects

public final class Effects
extends java.lang.Object

Small helpers for the properties that paint a stacking context as one group: filter (CSS Filter Effects 1), mix-blend-mode and isolation (CSS Compositing and Blending Level 1, https://www.w3.org/TR/compositing-1/) and the mask properties (CSS Masking Level 1). Such a box is a stacking context; a box with a filter is also the containing block of its absolutely positioned and fixed descendants (Filter Effects 1 section 5.1).

쌓임 맥락을 한 그룹으로 그리게 하는 속성을 위한 작은 도우미: filter(CSS Filter Effects 1), mix-blend-mode, isolation (CSS Compositing and Blending Level 1, https://www.w3.org/TR/compositing-1/), 마스크 속성(CSS Masking Level 1). 그런 상자는 쌓임 맥락이며, 필터가 있는 상자는 자신의 절대 배치와 fixed 자손의 포함 블록이기도 하다(Filter Effects 1 5.1 절).
