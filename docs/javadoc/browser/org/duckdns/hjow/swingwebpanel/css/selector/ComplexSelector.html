org.duckdns.hjow.swingwebpanel.css.selector
Class ComplexSelector

java.lang.Object
  org.duckdns.hjow.swingwebpanel.css.selector.ComplexSelector

public final class ComplexSelector
extends java.lang.Object

A complex selector: compound selectors joined by combinators (descendant, child, next-sibling, subsequent-sibling), for example nav > ul li + li. A relative complex selector (the argument of :has()) additionally has a leading combinator that relates its first compound selector to the anchor element.

복합 선택자: 결합자(자손, 자식, 다음 형제, 이후 형제)로 이어진 복합 선택자들. 예: nav > ul li + li. 상대 복합 선택자(:has() 의 인수)는 첫 복합 선택자와 기준 요소의 관계를 나타내는 맨 앞 결합자가 더 있다.
