Package org.duckdns.hjow.swingwebpanel.media.audio.pcm

Class Summary
AdpcmSource Decoder of the two block-based ADPCM codecs of WAV files: IMA/DVI ADPCM (format tag 0x0011) and Microsoft ADPCM (format tag 0x0002).
AiffDecoder Decoder of AIFF and AIFF-C files (plan item P18-07, FEATURE_MATRIX AUD-02): big-endian PCM of 1 to 32 bits, and the AIFF-C compression types NONE/twos, sowt (little-endian), raw (8-bit unsigned), in24, in32, fl32, fl64, ulaw, alaw and ima4 (QuickTime IMA ADPCM).
AuDecoder Decoder of Sun/NeXT AU (SND) files (plan item P18-07, FEATURE_MATRIX AUD-03): mu-law, A-law, linear PCM of 8, 16, 24 and 32 bits (big endian) and IEEE float of 32 and 64 bits.
RawPcmSource A PcmSource over interleaved samples that are stored in the encoded data itself (WAV, AIFF, AU): the samples are converted to 16 bits when they are read, so nothing is decoded in advance.
WavDecoder Decoder of RIFF/WAVE files, including the 64-bit forms RF64 and BW64 (plan item P18-07, FEATURE_MATRIX AUD-01): PCM of 8 (unsigned), 16, 24 and 32 bits, IEEE float of 32 and 64 bits, A-law, mu-law, IMA ADPCM and Microsoft ADPCM, also inside WAVE_FORMAT_EXTENSIBLE.
 

Enum Summary
SampleFormat The sample encodings of the uncompressed and companded audio formats (WAV, AIFF, AU).
